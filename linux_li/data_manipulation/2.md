
# Challenge 2 deleting characters
`tr` can delete characters also by the argument `-d` Now you give it a try. I'll intersperse some decoy characters (specifically: `^` and `%`) among the flag characters. Use `tr -d` to remove them!

## Solution:

we will simply invoke the command `/challenge/run | tr -d ^%
` which will give us the flag
### Commands Run:

```sh
hacker@data~deleting-characters:~$ /challenge/run | tr -d ^%
Your character-stuffed flag:

```
