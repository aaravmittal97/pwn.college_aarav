
# Challenge extracting specific sections of text 

In this challenge, the `/challenge/run` program will give you a bunch of lines with random numbers and single characters (characters of the flag) as columns. Use `cut` to extract the flag characters, then pipe them to `tr -d "\n"` (like the previous level!) to join them together into a single line. Your solution will look something like `/challenge/run | cut ??? | tr ???`, with the `???` filled out.

## Solution:

when we will run command `/challenge/run` it will give us random numbers with the single characters of flag,we will invoke command `/challenge/run | cut -d " " -f 2
` this will give us only the characters of flag which we will form in a single line by `/challenge/run | cut -d " " -f 2 | tr -d "\n"
` and we will get the flag
