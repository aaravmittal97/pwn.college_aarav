
# Challenge 4 extracting the first lines with head

This challenge's `/challenge/pwn` outputs a bunch of data, and you'll need to pipe it through `head` to grab just the first 7 lines and then pipe them onwards to `/challenge/college`, which will give you the flag if you do this right! Your solution will be a long composite command with _two_ pipes connecting three commands. Good luck!

## Solution:
we will firstly invoke the command `/challenge/pwn | head -n 7`
 which give us the first 7  outputs  which when we will pipe onwards to `/challenge/college` will give the flag.

### Commands Run:
```
hacker@data~extracting-the-first-lines-with-head:~$ /challenge/pwn | head -n 7
SECRET-CODE-19544
SECRET-CODE-16963
SECRET-CODE-16674
SECRET-CODE-19099

SECRET-CODE-6327
hacker@data~extracting-the-first-lines-with-head:~$  /challenge/pwn | head -n 7 | /challenge/college
Congratulations, you piped the right codes!
